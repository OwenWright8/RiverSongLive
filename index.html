Riversong, a timber-frame cabin above the river

Welcome to the Riversong Timber Frame Cabin, nestled in the quaint town of Leavenworth, Indiana. This retreat overlooks the Ohio River from the Indiana banks, facing the Kentucky landscape.

Welcome

Big windows, warm timber and the river at your feet

Riversong is a cozy timber-frame cabin built for two to four guests. Cook in a full kitchen, soak in the hot tub on the lower patio, gather around the fire pit, and watch the paddle wheelers and barges drift by below.
