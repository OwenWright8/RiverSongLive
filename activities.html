Or stay put

The best seat on the river

Some of the best moments happen right at the cabin: towboats pushing barges, paddle wheelers lit up at night, and sunsets that set the whole river glowing.

For a change of pace, historic Corydon has restaurants and shops about 25 minutes away, and Louisville is roughly 35 miles east.
